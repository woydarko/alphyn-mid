## Launch thread (copy/paste)
1/ DeFi has a transparency trap: to copy a good strategy you have to *see* it - and once it's visible on-chain, it gets front-run. The edge dies on reveal.

2/ Alphyn fixes this with zero-knowledge. Your AI-generated allocation is **private witness data** in a Compact smart contract on @MidnightNtwrk. Only a *commitment* (a hash) is public.

3/ Every rebalance recomputes that commitment from your private allocation and proves - in ZK - that the recorded PnL followed the strategy you committed to. A server could lie. A ZK circuit can't.

4/ The public ledger shows only **aggregates**: category, epoch count, cumulative PnL, Sharpe. Never the weights. Never the balance. `disclose()` is used on exactly three non-sensitive values - the compiler guarantees the rest can't leak.

5/ Social, without leaking: browse a leaderboard ranked by track record and **follow a vault's direction** - without either side's strategy ever being exposed. You follow the results, not the recipe.

6/ Built for the *New Moon to Full* Midnight builder program. Contract compiles to ZK circuits, tested, deploying to Preprod. Privacy is enforced by the compiler, not by trust.
